# Ponos site

Support page and privacy policy for the Ponos iOS app, served by GitHub Pages
at https://ponosapp.dev/ (privacy policy at `/privacy/`; `CNAME` names the
domain). The app itself lives in the private `gym-log` repo.

Plain HTML and one stylesheet, no build step. The palette follows the app's
`theme.config.js`, and `icon.png` / `favicon.png` come from its
`assets/images/` (rendered by `scripts/build-icons.mjs` there).

When the privacy policy changes, update its effective date.
